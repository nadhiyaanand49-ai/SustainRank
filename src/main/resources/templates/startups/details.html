Assessment Methodology

SustainRank evaluates early-stage ventures against an objective ESG matrix:

  • Environmental (25%): Renewable energy, carbon reduction, and circular manufacturing.
  • Innovation (25%): Intellectual property, patent pipeline, and technical moat.
  • Social Impact (25%): Beneficiaries reached and UN SDG alignment.
  • Financial Viability (25%): Capital efficiency, runway, and unit economics.
